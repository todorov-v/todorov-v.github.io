Projects & Case Studies

Selected real-world projects in virtualization, automation and disaster recovery.

VMware to Nutanix Migration

Migrated 100+ virtual machines with near zero-downtime, standardized images, and optimized performance. Redesigned backup policies and disaster recovery strategy for improved resilience and restore time.

  • VM migration and validation (compute & storage)
  • Backup redesign and Commvault policy standardization
  • DR workflows optimized for RTO/RPO targets

VM Provisioning Automation & Day-2 Operations

Implemented full lifecycle automation using VMware Aria for Day-0 provisioning and AWX (Ansible Tower) for Day-2 operations. The result — consistent, version-controlled deployments and automated configuration drift management.

  • Aria blueprints and extensibility actions
  • AWX playbooks for patching, backup, and lifecycle operations
  • Integrated GitOps workflows and dynamic IP assignment

Disaster Recovery Orchestration (SRM + Aria)

Combined VMware Site Recovery Manager (SRM) with Aria Automation to achieve consistent, repeatable, and monitored DR workflows. Automated recovery plans reduced manual intervention and guaranteed predictable failover testing.

  • SRM recovery plan automation via Aria extensibility
  • Automated protection group updates and monitoring
  • Runbooks integrated into Aria workflows
